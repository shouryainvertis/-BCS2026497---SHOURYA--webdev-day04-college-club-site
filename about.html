About the club

Started by students,
open to everyone.

Campus Connect began as a small group of first-year students who wanted a friendlier way to meet people and learn new skills outside the classroom.

Our story

What started as five friends sharing notes became a club with members from every department. We hold workshops, social evenings and larger events, and everything is organised by students.

We believe a good club should be easy to join, easy to understand and easy to help with.

What we value

  • Everyone is welcome, whatever their course or year.
  • Learning is shared, not competitive.
  • Events are free for members.
  • Every voice helps shape what we do next.

How to take part

  1. Sign up on the registration page.
  2. Come to your first meetup.
  3. Pick a team you would like to help.
