THE ABHIRUCHI COMMUNITY

Seven Clubs. Endless Possibilities.

Explore the seven sub-clubs of Invertis University's Abhiruchi.

🎨

01. Panache

The Arts Club

Panache encourages artistic expression through painting, modelling, sculpture, origami and other creative activities. It provides opportunities to showcase imagination through exhibitions, workshops and competitions.

Focus: Art • Painting • Creativity

🎭

02. Rock On

The Cultural Club

Rock On provides a platform for students passionate about singing, music, dancing and acting. It encourages students to develop their performing arts talents and express themselves on stage.

Focus: Singing • Dance • Acting

💻

03. I-Tech

The Technical Club

I-Tech brings together students interested in technology, coding, robotics and innovation. It promotes learning through workshops, seminars and exploration of emerging technologies such as AI and IoT.

Focus: Coding • Robotics • Technology

📸

04. Images

The Publication Club

Images is a creative platform for students interested in writing, public speaking and photography. It helps students express ideas, share stories and showcase university activities.

Focus: Writing • Photography • Speaking

🏅

05. Stride

The Sports Club

Stride promotes sports, physical fitness, teamwork and sportsmanship. It encourages participation in indoor and outdoor sporting activities and events such as Invertis Khel Mahotsav.

Focus: Fitness • Sports • Teamwork

📊

06. M-Factor

The Management Club

M-Factor develops leadership, strategic thinking and managerial skills. Activities include management games, case study competitions, business quizzes and workshops.

Focus: Leadership • Business • Management

🌱

07. The Responsible Invertian

The Social Club

The Responsible Invertian (RI) encourages students to contribute to society through social welfare initiatives. It focuses on supporting needy communities and creating positive social and environmental change.

Focus: Social Service • Awareness • Responsibility
