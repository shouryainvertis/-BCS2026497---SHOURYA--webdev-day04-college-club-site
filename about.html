About Abhiruchi

Where passion meets creativity and talent meets opportunity.

ABHIRUCHI – THE HOBBY CLUB

A Journey Beyond Academics

Abhiruchi is the hobby club ecosystem of Invertis University, Bareilly. Established on 3 October 2009, it provides students with opportunities to explore their hobbies, express creativity and develop their personal and professional skills.

Abhiruchi operates through seven diverse sub-clubs. Each club focuses on a different interest and gives students a platform to participate in activities beyond classroom learning.

🌟

Our Philosophy

Every student has a unique talent. Abhiruchi encourages students to discover it, develop it and share it with others.
